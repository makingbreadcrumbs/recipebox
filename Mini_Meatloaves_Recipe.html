Favorite Mini Meatloaves

Meatloaf baked in a muffin pan with two hidden veggies — 30 minutes start to finish, and it freezes beautifully.

Serves
9
Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Difficulty
Easy

Baking meatloaf in a muffin pan cuts the cook time from an hour-plus down to twenty minutes, and the portions come out toddler-sized without any slicing. Grated onion and carrot melt into the mix, adding nutrients without changing the texture or flavor enough for anyone to object. It's iron-rich, reheats well, and freezes for up to six months — so it doubles as a make-ahead freezer meal. Serve with buttered noodles, rice, or roasted potatoes.

The Meatloaf

Everything mixed in one bowl
  • Lean ground beef (or turkey/chicken)1 lb
  • Breadcrumbs, Italian-style1/2 cup
  • Onion, grated1/4 cup
  • Carrot, grated1/4 cup
  • Egg, lightly beaten1

Grate the onion and carrot on a box grater so they disappear into the mix.

Seasoning & Topping

Ketchup goes on last, not in the bowl
  • Dijon mustard1 tbsp
  • Garlic powder1/2 tsp
  • Chili powder1/2 tsp
  • Salt1/2 tsp
  • Ketchup, for brushing1/4 cup
Favorite Mini Meatloaves 01

Directions

Mix & Fill
  1. 1Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a nonstick muffin pan with nonstick spray.
  2. 2Add all the ingredients except the ketchup to a large bowl and mix well with clean hands or a spoon.
  3. 3Divide the mixture among the muffin cups, about 1/4 cup each, and press down gently.
  4. 4Brush the top of each with ketchup.
Bake & Serve
  1. 1Bake for 20–24 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center reads 160°F.
  2. 2Let cool 2 minutes in the pan, then loosen with a knife if needed and transfer to a plate.
  3. 3Serve warm with the sides of your choice.

Tips & Variations

  • Use a thermometer rather than the clock — pulling them right at 160°F is what keeps them juicy instead of dry.
  • Swap minced mushrooms for the carrot if you'd rather the veggies blend in without the orange flecks.
  • Gluten-free: use GF breadcrumbs. Egg-free: omit the egg and add a splash of milk instead.
  • Keeps 5 days in the fridge, or freeze cooked meatloaves up to 6 months with the air pressed out of the bag.
