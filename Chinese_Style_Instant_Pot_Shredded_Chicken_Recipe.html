Chinese-Style Instant Pot Shredded Chicken

A meal-prep staple — juicy shredded chicken in a savory oyster sauce, ready in about 20 minutes of Instant Pot time.

Serves
4–6
Prep
5 min
Cook
15 min
Difficulty
Easy

This Chinese-style shredded chicken leans on oyster sauce instead of the honey or bbq sauce found in most Instant Pot chicken recipes, giving it a savory, umami-rich coating rather than a sweet one. Mix the sauce right in the pot, add the chicken, and let the Instant Pot do the work — a big batch keeps in the fridge for days and freezes well, making it an easy Sunday meal-prep staple for rice bowls, noodle soups, salads, and wraps all week.

The Sauce

Mixed directly in the Instant Pot
  • Oyster sauce1/4 cup
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar1 tbsp
  • Ginger, grated1 tsp
  • Garlic, grated2 cloves
  • Ground black pepper1/4 tsp

The Chicken

Pressure-cooked, then shredded
  • Boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)2 lbs
  • Green onion, chopped, for garnish (optional)to taste

Thighs stay juicier if slightly overcooked; breasts give leaner, cleaner shreds.

Chinese-Style Instant Pot Shredded Chicken 01

Directions

Mix & Cook
  1. 1Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and black pepper directly into the Instant Pot. Stir to combine.
  2. 2Add the chicken and toss to coat with the sauce, spreading the pieces out with minimal overlap.
  3. 3Close the lid, seal the valve, select manual/pressure cook on high, and set the timer for 10 minutes (7–8 minutes for smaller breasts).
Release & Shred
  1. 1When cooking is done, let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then quick release the rest.
  2. 2Open the lid and let cool a few minutes, then shred the chicken directly in the pot with two forks.
  3. 3Toss the shredded chicken with the sauce until coated. Serve hot over rice or noodles, or let cool and store for the week.

Tips & Variations

  • Chicken thighs are more forgiving for meal prep — they stay tender even if the timing runs slightly over.
  • Frozen chicken works fine — add it straight from frozen and increase the pressure cook time to 15 minutes.
  • If the sauce is thin after shredding, switch to sauté and stir in a cornstarch slurry to thicken it in about a minute.
