Simple Miso Ramen

A clean, minimalist miso ramen you can pull together in 15 minutes flat.

Serves
2
Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Difficulty
Easy

A pared-back weeknight miso ramen built on dashi, sesame oil, and a handful of pantry staples. No long simmer, no fussy prep — just a warm, savory bowl in under 15 minutes. Great as a base recipe to customize with whatever toppings you have on hand.

Broth

The soup base
  • Dashi stock4 cups
  • Miso paste (white or red)3 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp
  • Garlic, minced2 cloves
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Mirin1 tbsp

Water plus instant dashi granules works in place of fresh dashi stock.

Noodles & Toppings

Cook noodles in the broth, add toppings when plating
  • Ramen noodles (fresh or dried)2 servings
  • Green onions, slicedto taste
  • Corn kernelsto taste
  • Boiled eggto taste
Simple Miso Ramen 01

Directions

Build the Broth
  1. 1Heat sesame oil in a pot over medium heat. Cook minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. 2Pour in dashi stock, soy sauce, and mirin. Bring to a gentle boil.
  3. 3Turn heat down to low. Add ramen noodles and cook until soft — about 3 minutes for fresh, 4–5 minutes for dried.
Finish & Serve
  1. 1Place miso paste in a small ladle, dip into the warm broth, and stir with chopsticks until fully dissolved. Do not let the soup boil after adding miso.
  2. 2Divide noodles and broth among bowls. Add green onions, corn, boiled egg, and any other toppings. Serve immediately.

Tips & Variations

  • Always dissolve miso off the boil — high heat kills the live cultures and dulls the flavor.
  • For a soft-boiled egg topping, boil for 6.5 minutes, then ice-bath immediately. Halve when plating.
  • Add a knob of butter to each bowl for a richer, creamier broth (miso butter ramen style).
