One-Pot Toddler Pasta

A simple, one-pot pasta cooked right in broth for a creamy, kid-approved meal.

Serves
4
Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Difficulty
Easy

Cooking the pasta directly in chicken bone broth gives it a flavor somewhere between chicken noodle soup and mac and cheese, and shredded carrot and peas sneak in extra veggies without a fuss. It comes together in one pot in about 15 minutes, making it a reliable go-to for toddlers and picky eaters alike.

Pasta & Veggies

Cooked together in one pot
  • Alphabet pasta (or any short-cut pasta)4 oz
  • Chicken bone broth1⅓ cups
  • Carrot, shredded1
  • Frozen peas½ cup

Regular broth, stock, or even water works if you don't have bone broth on hand.

Finishing

Stirred in once pasta is tender
  • Butter1 tbsp
  • Heavy cream1 tbsp
  • White cheddar, shredded1 oz
One-Pot Toddler Pasta 01

Directions

Cook the Pasta
  1. 1Add pasta, bone broth, carrot, and peas to a medium-sized pot over medium-high heat.
  2. 2Bring to a boil. Stir frequently until the pasta is al dente, about 8–10 minutes.
Finish & Serve
  1. 1Stir in the butter, cream, and cheese until well-combined and creamy.
  2. 2Add salt and pepper, if desired, and serve immediately.

Tips & Variations

  • Try protein-packed chickpea pasta, like Banza, for added nutrients.
  • If there's leftover liquid in the pot after cooking, simmer a minute or two longer, or drain slightly before stirring in the cheese.
  • Grate in a bit of broccoli along with the carrot to sneak in even more veggies.
