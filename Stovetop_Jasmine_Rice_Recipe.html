15-Minute Stovetop Jasmine Rice

A quick, buttery stovetop rice with only five ingredients — the side dish that goes with everything.

Serves
3–4
Prep
1 min
Cook
14 min
Difficulty
Easy

This is the go-to stovetop jasmine rice for pairing with just about any dinner. The ingredients are basic, but two small moves make it far better than the store-bought pre-cooked stuff: toasting the rinsed rice in butter for a couple of minutes first, and swapping some of the water for chicken broth. It takes barely any prep, and it's the natural partner for the Korean ground beef bowls or the air fryer salmon.

The Rice

Rinsed, then toasted in butter
  • Jasmine rice1 cup
  • Unsalted butter1/4 tbsp

Rinse the rice well, until the water running through is clear — this removes excess starch and keeps the grains separate.

Liquid & Seasoning

Added once the rice is toasted
  • Water1 cup
  • Chicken broth or bone broth1/2 cup
  • Saltto taste
15-Minute Stovetop Jasmine Rice 01

Directions

Toast
  1. 1Bring a small pot to medium heat and melt the butter.
  2. 2Rinse the jasmine rice, then add it to the pot and stir with the butter for 2–3 minutes to give it a light toast.
Steam & Season
  1. 1Add the water and chicken broth, cover the pot, and turn the heat to high.
  2. 2Once it comes to a boil, drop the heat to its lowest setting and let the rice steam, covered, for about 14 minutes.
  3. 3Season with salt to taste, fluff, and serve.

Tips & Variations

  • No broth on hand? Use 1 1/2 cups water total instead, and add a chicken bouillon cube for the flavor you'd otherwise lose.
  • Keep the lid on for the full 14 minutes — lifting it lets the steam out and leaves the rice unevenly cooked.
  • Stores in a sealed container in the fridge for 3–4 days.
