Instant Pot Braised Chicken

A quick Chinese-style braised chicken in a dark soy and 5-spice sauce, pressure-cooked in under 40 minutes.

Serves
5
Prep
5 min
Cook
17 min
Difficulty
Easy

Inspired by Cynthia's mom's stovetop version, this Chinese-style braised chicken gets a savory-sweet glaze from dark soy sauce, brown sugar, and Chinese 5-spice, then finishes with quick-cooked vegetables and a cornstarch-thickened sauce. The Instant Pot turns a 30–45 minute simmer into about 12 minutes of pressure cooking, and it reheats well for lunch the next day.

The Chicken

Marinated, then browned and pressure cooked
  • Chicken drumsticks (or bone-in thighs)10
  • Dark soy sauce1/4 cup
  • Water1/4 cup
  • Chinese 5-spice3 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp
  • Light soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Granulated garlic (or garlic powder)1 tsp
  • Brown sugar1/4 cup
  • White pepper (optional)to taste

Brown sugar is optional if you're watching sugar intake — the dish still works without it.

Vegetables & Finishing

Added after pressure cooking the chicken
  • Broccoli, cut into florets1/4 cup
  • Carrots, peeled and chopped2
  • Bell pepper, chopped1
  • Vegetable oil, for browning1–2 tbsp
  • Cornstarch, for the slurry1 tbsp
Instant Pot Braised Chicken 01

Directions

Marinate & Brown
  1. 1Marinate the chicken with the dark soy sauce, brown sugar, light soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil, Chinese 5-spice, and pepper. Let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. 2Set the Instant Pot to sauté and wait until it says hot. Add 1–2 tbsp vegetable oil.
  3. 3Add the marinated chicken to the pot (reserve the leftover sauce) and brown on all sides, turning as needed — it's fine to pile pieces on top of each other.
Pressure Cook & Finish
  1. 1Cancel sauté. Add the reserved sauce and 1/4 cup water. Close the lid, set the vent to sealing, and cook on manual pressure for 10 minutes.
  2. 2Start rice separately (about 1/4 cup per person) so it finishes around the same time as the chicken.
  3. 3When the 10 minutes is up, carefully quick-release the pressure, add the vegetables, then cook on manual pressure for 2 more minutes.
  4. 4Let sit 5 minutes, then quick-release again. Transfer the chicken and vegetables to a serving dish deep enough to hold the sauce.
  5. 5Whisk the cornstarch with a small splash of cold water to make a slurry. Hit sauté and stir the slurry into the sauce until thickened, then pour over the chicken and serve.

Tips & Variations

  • No Instant Pot? Simmer covered on the stovetop for 30–45 minutes instead of pressure cooking.
  • No Chinese 5-spice on hand? Swap in fresh or powdered ginger, garlic, and a bit more soy sauce.
  • Swap in whatever vegetables are in season — soaked dried shiitake mushrooms are a great addition if you have them.
