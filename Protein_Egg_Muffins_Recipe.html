Protein Egg Muffins

Eggs whipped with cottage cheese and baked in a muffin tin — high protein, endlessly customizable, done in 15 minutes.

Makes
6
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Difficulty
Easy

Whisking cottage cheese into the eggs is what makes these puff up like little soufflés and gives them the protein to actually hold you over. This is a half batch — six muffins, one per cup — with bacon and sautéed red pepper folded in, though the add-ins are entirely up to whatever's in the fridge. Fifteen minutes in the toaster oven at 350°F set them perfectly: golden on top, fully cooked through the middle, and no watery weeping in the pan.

The Base

Whisked together in a bowl
  • Eggs6
  • Cottage cheese1/4 cup
  • Olive oil, for greasing the tinto coat

A quarter cup for six eggs runs leaner than the original's tablespoon-per-cup — that's what keeps them from going watery.

Add-Ins

Added after the cups are filled
  • Bacon, cooked and diced2 slices
  • Red bell pepper, diced1/4
  • Shredded Mexican cheese blenda good pinch each

Swap freely — spinach, tomatoes, mushrooms, or ham all work.

Protein Egg Muffins 01

Directions

Prep the Add-Ins
  1. 1Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a muffin tin with olive oil.
  2. 2Cook the bacon on the stovetop, set aside to cool, then dice.
  3. 3Dice the bell pepper and sauté it briefly in the same pan, in the bacon fat.
Fill & Bake
  1. 1Whisk the eggs and cottage cheese together in a bowl until evenly combined.
  2. 2Fill each muffin cup about 75% full with the egg mixture — they rise a lot.
  3. 3Divide the bacon and bell pepper evenly between the cups, then top each with a good pinch of shredded cheese.
  4. 4Bake about 15 minutes, until puffed, golden, and fully set in the center.

Tips & Variations

  • 15 minutes is the toaster-oven time. A full-size oven runs cooler at the edges — start checking at 15 but expect closer to 20.
  • Whisking the base in a bowl rather than mixing it cup-by-cup with a fork gives a much more even, uniform texture.
  • Don't overfill. At 75% they crest well above the rim while baking and settle back down as they cool.
  • Keep 4 days in the fridge or freeze up to 2 months. Reheat 20–30 seconds in the microwave.
