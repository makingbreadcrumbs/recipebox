Southwest Ground Beef and Rice Skillet

A 40-minute one-skillet dinner built on pantry cans and leftover rice — set out toppings and let everyone build their own bowl.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
30 min
Difficulty
Easy

This is the weeknight answer to a pound of ground beef and some leftover rice. Almost everything else comes out of cans — tomatoes, corn, beans — so it's the kind of dinner you can pull together without a shopping trip. Everything happens in one skillet, the cheese melts under a lid at the end, and the real move is setting out bowls of toppings so everyone builds their own. Leftover jasmine rice from earlier in the week is perfect here.

Beef & Seasoning

Browned first, spices bloomed after
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Ground beef1 lb
  • Onion, small, diced1
  • Garlic cloves, minced2
  • Chili powder1 tsp
  • Ground cumin1/2 tsp
  • Smoked paprika1/2 tsp
  • Onion powder1/2 tsp
  • Garlic powder1/2 tsp
  • Cayenne pepper (optional)1/4 tsp
  • Kosher salt & black pepperto taste

Stir-Ins & Topping

Mostly pantry cans
  • Diced tomatoes, undrained14.5 oz
  • Corn kernels, drained15.25 oz
  • Black or pinto beans, drained15 oz
  • Cooked rice2 cups
  • Shredded Mexican cheese blend1 cup

Toppings to set out: green onions, cilantro, a squeeze of lime, tortilla chips, salsa, guacamole, avocado, sour cream, jalapeños.

Directions

Brown & Season
  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add the ground beef, onion, and garlic. Cook, stirring and breaking up the meat, until no longer pink — 5 to 7 minutes. Drain off the excess grease.
  2. 2Reduce the heat to medium. Add all the spices plus about 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper. Cook, stirring, about 30 seconds to bloom them.
Simmer & Melt
  1. 1Add the tomatoes with their juices, the corn, and the beans. Bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low and cook about 5 minutes.
  2. 2Stir in the rice — a fork works better than a spoon here, since it separates the grains instead of clumping them. If it looks dry, loosen with a splash of water, broth, or salsa. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  3. 3Sprinkle the cheese over the top, cover, and cook 2–3 minutes until melted and bubbly. For browned, crispier cheese, run the skillet under the broiler instead.
  4. 4Serve in bowls and let everyone pile on their own toppings.

Tips & Variations

  • Any cooked rice works — leftovers, a microwave pouch, white, brown, or jasmine. If you use cauliflower rice, drain it well or it waters the whole dish down.
  • Skip the freezer if you can. It keeps 3 months, but rice swells as it freezes and thaws out mushy — 3 to 4 days in the fridge is the better plan.
  • Reheat gently in a covered skillet over low heat, just until warm. Push it too far and the rice turns gummy.
  • Swap the beef for ground turkey, chicken, pork, or sausage. A splash of Worcestershire adds depth; a teaspoon of sugar balances the acidity of the tomatoes.
